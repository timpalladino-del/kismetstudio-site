Par is the truth

Every unit has a par: the fewest presses it can possibly be done in, worked out by the game itself. Match it for three stars. Undo and Clear are free, so experiment. There are no timers, no lives and no fail state.

The machine resolves left to right with no order of operations. On this calculator, 3 + 4 × 2 is 14.

One new rule a chapter

The first four chapters teach the instrument. Each chapter after that changes one thing: a key that fires twice, a key that dies after two uses, an equals that repeats itself, a screen that goes dark until you press it.

Word Mode asks a question. You answer by typing the number that reads as the word when the screen is upside down. 5508 is BOSS.

Hints that cost something

A nudge shows your next press. A route shows the whole solution. Both cost gold and cap the stars you can earn on that unit, permanently, so a hint is a choice rather than a shortcut. Revealing a word is free.

Buy it or earn it

Each chapter is a single purchase, or 4,000 gold. Play pays gold by the stars you reach, and every unit in a chapter at three stars pays exactly the next chapter. Perfect play is free. Skins come in packs of six you can try on before buying.

Support

I bought a chapter or skin pack and it is goneOpen the Store, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and come back on any device signed into it.
My gold or progress did not follow me to a new phoneGold, unlocked chapters and progress sync through iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive on, then open Sumhow on the new device and wait a minute.
A price button is greyThe App Store did not answer when the store opened, usually a connection issue. Close the Store sheet and open it again.
I want a refundApple handles all refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.
Music or sound stoppedSumhow stays silent while another app is playing audio, on purpose. Both have their own switches on the home screen under Everything else.
I am stuck on a unitClear is free; try a different first key. If you want help, a nudge costs 20 gold and shows the next press on the shortest route.
Something elseEmail us. A real person reads it, usually the same day.
