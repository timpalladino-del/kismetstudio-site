CastleWise

Home maintenance for iPhone
Lifestyle · Home Better at life

The owner's manual your house never came with.

You own a house, and nobody handed you the manual. So the filter gets changed when you happen to remember, the water heater warranty is in a drawer somewhere, and every strange noise starts with a search bar at 9 pm.

CastleWise fixes that. Keep every appliance, warranty, and service date in one Binder. Get reminded before the filter, the gutters, or the flush is due, not after. When something breaks, point your camera at it with FixLens and follow one of 120 plain-English playbooks before you call anyone. Eleven calculators handle the paint, the mulch, and the BTUs. You stop guessing, skip the repairs you could have prevented, and finally feel like you run the house instead of the other way around.

Sumhow

Number puzzle for iPhone
Games · Puzzle Brain workout

Three keys. One number. Somehow, you'll get there.

If you do the Wordle before your coffee, this is your next habit. Sumhow hands you a calculator with most of its keys missing, names a number, and asks you to reach it in the fewest presses. Level one gives you a 3, a plus, and a times, and asks for 99. You already know how to work a calculator, so there is no tutorial. The first puzzle clicks in about four seconds.

Every chapter changes the keypad on you. A key that types twice. A key that is slowly dying. A mirror that flips your digits. No timers, no lives, no fail state, just you, a broken little machine, and that small rush when the number lands. And some numbers make the machine do things we are not going to explain here.

Tools that bring a little joy and ease to your day.

Kismet Studio is a small independent studio that builds apps for iPhone. Some are there to make life more fun while giving your brain a proper workout. Others make you better at the things you already do, at work, at home, and everywhere in between.

Every app follows the same rule. It earns its place on your phone by doing one job well, respecting your time and your data, and still working next year. And we never call one finished. Every app keeps getting better, with new features and smarter ways to do what it already does.

Kismet is the old word for a lucky turn of fate. That is the feeling we are after: the right small tool turning up at exactly the right moment.
