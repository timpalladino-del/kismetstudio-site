Sumhow Privacy Policy

Short version: Sumhow does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Effective 15 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Sumhow has no sign-in, no analytics software and no advertising software. We do not know who you are, and we do not want to.

Where your progress lives

Your solved units, stars, gold balance and unlocked chapters are saved on your device. If your iPhone is signed into iCloud, the same information is stored in your own iCloud key-value storage so it follows you to your other devices. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. Kismet Studio LLC cannot read it, and Apple's iCloud terms govern it.

Purchases

Chapters, gold and skin packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, receipts and refunds. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers those transactions.

Children

Sumhow is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that mattered would be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: team@kismetstudio.io.
